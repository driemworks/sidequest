# What Sidequest is betting on

**The bet (2026-09-26):** players will use a recommender that answers "what should I play
next?" in their own words ("something gritty and atmospheric") and ranks by their real
play history, if its picks are ones they would actually enjoy. No accounts; the player's
history never leaves their device.

**Decide by 2027-03-31.** It fails if a player acts on a pick (buys or plays it) and hates
it often enough to stop trusting it: one bad purchase is immediate disrepute.

## any-player

Any Steam player deciding what to play next, whatever their taste: no genre or mood is
favoured, so the questions are spread across the kinds of player.

- Questions: a mood or feel ("gritty and atmospheric", "something relaxing"), a genre
  (competitive shooter, grand strategy, JRPG, racing sim), a length ("finish in a
  weekend"), who with ("with my partner"), and "games like <a game I loved>".
- Needs: every game's community tags, a short description, review scores, and a length
  where Steam has playtime.
- Covers: the Steam catalog back to its start, limited only by what a browser tab can load
  (v1: games with at least 10 reviews).
- Fails if: a search for a feel returns games whose tags do not have that feel.

## safe-by-default

Anyone, with no login and no age check.

- Must never show: adult sexual content or nudity. Those titles are dropped before anything
  is staged, because every published record is public and permanent. An opt-in adult view
  waits for gated access.
